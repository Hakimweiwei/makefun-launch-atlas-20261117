REPRODUCIBLE BY DESIGN

Every number has a trail.

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × maturity factor. Category multiples: DEX / derivatives 1.20; lending / credit / CDP 0.85; yield / farming / staking 0.60; bridge / wrapped / CEX 0.45; other 0.75.

Growth factor = 1 + seven-day TVL change, clipped to ±50%. Maturity = 0.75 + 0.50 × min(age in days / 730, 1). Age starts when DefiLlama began tracking, not protocol launch. Missing growth is neutral; missing age receives 0.90. Values are rounded dollars, ranked descending, with slug ties. The 0.5×–1.5× range is a scenario envelope, not a statistical confidence interval. These multiples are modeling assumptions, not observed market prices.
